Keep shoes in use

KleanKickx is Accra's sneaker care and collection hub. We extend the life of shoes, train the people who restore them, and make sure the pairs we can't save don't end up in landfill.

22 billion pairs discarded globally

Every year, about 22 billion pairs of shoes are sent to landfill or burned, out of roughly 24 billion made.

What we do

  • Extend: every Klean adds wear to a pair that already exists.
  • Train: young people in Accra learn technical cleaning, restoration and triage.
  • Recover: pairs past saving go to our partner KicksCTRL.

Accra pilot with KicksCTRL

Over two days at one collection point: 682 records taken in, 446 pairs back into use, and 100% of records sent to reuse or held as recovery feedstock.

Trade-In

Bring the pairs you no longer wear. We check every one by hand: wearable pairs are cleaned and go back into use, and pairs past saving go to our partner KicksCTRL for material recovery, not landfill.
